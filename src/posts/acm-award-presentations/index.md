At ACM last week the retiring President, Janet Selley presented the Napier Trophy to Swimmer of the Year, Ella Swete for her performance at the Roma Junior Diving Cup. Also at the meeting, the Jan Alderman Memorial Trophy was awarded to Matthew Roberts, coach of the year. Both are from Southampton Diving Academy. Pictured is Ella with her Dad, Dave Swete, Chairman of Southampton Diving Academy, who accepted the trophy on behalf of Matthew, who was coaching.
