The Hampshire and Channel Islands Masters County Championships took place on **20th June 2026** at Hart Leisure Centre, Hart. Full results can be found below.

Day.1 of the Hampshire and Channel Islands Masters County Championships took place on **12th April 2026** at The Aquadrome, Basingstoke. Full results can be found here:

[Masters Weekend 1 - Results](/assets/uploads/2026/05/Masters-Weekend-1-Results.pdf)[Download](/assets/uploads/2026/05/Masters-Weekend-1-Results.pdf)

Congratulations to South Downs Trojans for taking the Top Club Aggregate Trophy for the first time, but a very slender margin over Basingstoke Bluefins (less than three points!) The Bluefins did however near clean-sweep the six relay divisions, spare one, and successfully walk away with both the Top Man and Top Women events.

TOP CLUB:

- South Downs Trojans : 413pts

- Basingstoke Bluefins : 410.5pts

- City of Southampton : 114pts

TOP MAN : Spencer Turner (Basingstoke Bluefins - 55-59yrs)

TOP WOMAN : Fleur Turner (Basingstoke Bluefins - 50-54yrs)

RELAY RESULTS :

- Medley 72yrs+ : South Downs Trojans

- Medley 120yrs+ : Basingstoke Bluefins

- Medley 200yrs+ : Basingstoke Bluefins

- FC 72yrs+ : Basingstoke Bluefins

- FC 120yrs+ : Basingstoke Bluefins

- FC 200yrs+ : Basingstoke Bluefins

SKINS EVENT : Mens Champion - Martin Bennell (Basingstoke Bluefins - 45-49yrs) / Womens Champion - Lizzie Coster (Basingstoke Bluefins).
