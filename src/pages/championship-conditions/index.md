**DIVING CHAMPIONSHIP CONDITIONS**

1. All diving installations shall be in accordance with the present FINA regulations and approved by a representative of the  Swim England Diving Committee.

- The tests shall be set out in the specific sections below.  All dives to be selected from the FINA tables and no dive to be repeated other than dives specific to Novice and Skills Competitions.  Dives of the same number to count as the same dive.  Dive sheets must be in the hands of the Diving Secretary 1 hour before competition.  Changes to Dive Sheets may be made up to half an hour before the start of the competition.

- Details of the entry on the official form shall be in the hands of the Diving Secretary 21 days before the championship.  Late entries will be accepted up to one hour before the commencement of the event, dependant on numbers, and subject to a payment of double entry fee.

- There shall be a referee, who shall be in charge of the Championship

- General Championship Conditions of the County Association and conditions of the Swim England Diving Championships shall apply.

- A junior shall be defined as anyone 18 years of age or under in the year of the event.

- Divers must be a member of a club affiliated to the Hampshire ASA.
