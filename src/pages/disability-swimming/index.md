Hampshire County ASA is committed to providing opportunities for swimmers of all abilities. Disability swimming in Hampshire is supported through Swim England's regional programmes and affiliated clubs.

If you are looking for information on disability swimming opportunities in Hampshire, please [contact us](/contact-us/) or visit the [Swim England website](https://www.swimming.org/swimengland/disability-swimming/).
