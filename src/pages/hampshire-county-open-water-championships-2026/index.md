 Open Swimming Announcement 

We are happy to announce Open Swimming is back in the County programme and will be incorporated in the South East Region Open Water Championships.

It will be held at Mercers Lake which is situated in Mercers Park, Merstham, Nr. Redhill, Surrey on Sunday 28th June

Full championships conditions for age groups and masters together with entry form please follow the link on the opening page of website.

Good luck to everyone entering. The Hampshire President will be at the event to present medals to the winners.
