**Hale Cup, Presented by Councillor W Hale**

The Hale Cup shall be awarded to the Club whose members gain the highest aggregate of points in the County 15 and Over Swimming Championships in any one year.  If two or more clubs gain the same number of points such clubs shall hold the trophy jointly.

Points shall be awarded, in each event in the 15/Over Championships, including relays, as follows:  
1st - 8 points; 2nd - 7 points; 3rd - 6 points; 4th - 5 points; 5th - 4 points; 6th - 3 points, 7th – 2 points, 8th – 1 point.  
In dead heats, points shall be divided. No two clubs can make use of the same competitor, for the purpose of counting points, in this competition in any one year.

**The Age Group Swimming Aggregate Cup, Presented by Swimstop**

The Age Group Swimming Aggregate Cup shall be awarded to the Club whose members gain the highest aggregate of points in the County 14 and Under Swimming Championships in any one year.  If two or more clubs gain the same number of points such clubs shall hold the trophy jointly.

Points shall be awarded, in each event in the 14/Under Championships, including relays, as follows:  
1st - 8 points; 2nd - 7 points; 3rd - 6 points; 4th - 5 points; 5th - 4 points; 6th - 3 points, 7th – 2 points, 8th – 1 point.  
In dead heats, points shall be divided. No two clubs can make use of the same competitor, for the purpose of counting points, in this competition in any one year.

**Royal Aircraft Establishment Individual Male Age Group Aggregate Trophy**

The RAE Individual Male Age Group Swimming Aggregate Cup shall be awarded to the male swimmer gaining the highest aggregate of points in the County 14 and Under Swimming Championships in any one year.  If two or more swimmers gain the same number of points such clubs shall hold the trophy jointly.

Points shall be awarded, in each individual event in the 14/Under Championships, as follows:  
1st - 8 points; 2nd - 7 points; 3rd - 6 points; 4th - 5 points; 5th - 4 points; 6th - 3 points, 7th – 2 points, 8th – 1 point.  
In dead heats, points shall be divided.

**Royal Aircraft Establishment Individual Female Age Group Aggregate Trophy**

The RAE Individual Male Age Group Swimming Aggregate Cup shall be awarded to the female swimmer gaining the highest aggregate of points in the County 14 and Under Swimming Championships in any one year.  If two or more swimmers gain the same number of points such clubs shall hold the trophy jointly.

Points shall be awarded, in each individual event in the 14/Under Championships, as follows:  
1st - 8 points; 2nd - 7 points; 3rd - 6 points; 4th - 5 points; 5th - 4 points; 6th - 3 points, 7th – 2 points, 8th – 1 point.  
In dead heats, points shall be divided.

**The Corinthian Cup, Presented by Lymington SC**

The Corinthian Cup shall be awarded to the Club whose members gain the highest aggregate of points in the County Junior Swimming Championships in any one year.  If two or more clubs gain the same number of points such clubs shall hold the trophy jointly.

Points shall be awarded, in each event in the Junior Championships, including relays, as follows:  
1st - 8 points; 2nd - 7 points; 3rd - 6 points; 4th - 5 points; 5th - 4 points; 6th - 3 points, 7th – 2 points, 8th – 1 point.  
In dead heats, points shall be divided. No two clubs can make use of the same competitor, for the purpose of counting points, in this competition in any one year.

Because of changes to the County Swimming Championships mandated by Swim England, the Association has not been able to run a Junior Swimming Championships since 2018, which means that this trophy, and the majority of the individual event trophies are not currently being awarded.
