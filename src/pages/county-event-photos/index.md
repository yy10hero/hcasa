Hampshire County ASA clubs and members are free to download these images for their own personal use and for use within their own club. All images remain copyright property of the County Association and may not be used for any other purpose without prior permission.

Higher resolution versions may be available.

To request permission to use images and/or to request a higher resolution version, contact [photos@hampshireswimming.org](mailto:photos@hampshireswimming.org)

We are keen to build a comprehensive archive of information and photos of county events. If you have any relevant photos you are prepared to submit, please send to [photos@hampshireswimming.org](mailto:photos@hampshireswimming.org)
