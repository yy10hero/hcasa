The 2023 County Swimming Championships were held as follows:

- Saturday/Sunday 21st/22nd January 2023

- Saturday/Sunday 4th/5th February 2023

- Saturday/Sunday 25th/26th February 2023

All events were held Long Course at Winchester Leisure Centre
